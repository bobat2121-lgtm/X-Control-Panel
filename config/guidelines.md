# Posting guidelines (guiding, not binding)

1. **Short posts** are either
   - an observation on current data or news, or
   - a meme-style post with a funny one-liner.
2. **Reposts / quote posts** always add something: an analytical point, a joke, or another observation. Never a bare repost or a restatement of the original.
3. **Analytical posts** don't have to be about today. They can cover current events, but they can also analyze a trend or a broader market move (weeks, months, cycles).

Mix: mostly short posts, with a few long analytical pieces.
